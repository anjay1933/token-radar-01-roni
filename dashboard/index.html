How this radar works

Model radar-3. Every implied value comes only from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day change, listing date, chains and category. No protocol name is special-cased; the same formula scores all 240 rows:

implied = TVL × 0.20 × growthF × chainF × categoryF × maturityF
growthF   = 1 + clamp(change_7d, −40, +100) / 100 × 0.8   (unknown change = 0)
chainF    = 1 + 0.03 × min(chains, 10)
categoryF = 0.85 + 0.30 × √(protocol TVL / total TVL of its category)
maturityF = 0.55 + 0.45 × ln(1 + min(age_days, 1460)) / ln(1461)   (unknown age = 365 days)

Source. Data: DefiLlama protocols snapshot, fetched 2026-11-14 (as_of 2026-11-14, floor $50M). The board holds the full 40-protocol watchlist plus the 200 largest pre-token protocols below the floor — 240 rows. The live-token tab shows the 24 largest market caps among protocols whose token already trades, CEX category excluded; their market caps are the market's own values and are never mixed with implied values.

Honesty. “Pre-token” means the snapshot records no token for the protocol — the record is not proof it has none. Costs and times in the farm plans are estimates. The snapshot carries no points or airdrop data, so this radar claims no specific points or airdrop program for any protocol; step 6 of every plan only tells you to check the protocol's official rewards page yourself.
